Yesterday I spoke at the
[Claude Operators Summit](https://www.communitystack.io/conferences/claude-operators-summit-london)
in London. Community Stack organises it as a single-track, in-person day for
teams building and running AI systems with Claude and related tools. Digital
Futures hosted the event at its venue on Worship Square.

Engineers, product leaders and AI operators shared their experience of running
AI in production. Talks covered agent workflows, context and memory, integration
with enterprise systems and operating AI responsibly at scale. The programme
also had keynotes, a recruitment panel and a set of short ignite talks.

My session had the half hour before lunch. Its title and abstract, as listed in
the programme:

## From first commit to self-improving agent: lessons from running Claude in production

> This talk follows one project, assembled from what Nearform’s teams have
> learned across several client engagements, with the goal of bringing Claude
> agents into an existing business workflow. It starts with model selection and
> cost optimisation in one engineer’s Claude Code setup, then moves to team
> collaboration, shared skills and codebase mapping. Guardrails and context
> management keep the agent on track, and caching keeps it affordable. Golden
> datasets and evals show whether a change helped, and telemetry from production
> feeds the next improvement. Along the way it looks at where a human needs to
> stay in the loop, and what evidence would let them step back, sharing what
> worked and what failed with concrete examples. It’s aimed at engineers building
> agents and at the people who decide whether they ship.
